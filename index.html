Take ten minutes. Don't reach for a finished thought." --- An opening prompt for ourselves and for anyone who wants to write alongside us. > Write about a thing you carry. Not a metaphor. An actual object. Where it came from, what it has been through with you, what it is doing in your life right now, what it has cost you to keep. Take ten minutes. Don't reach for a finished thought. Write past the place where you think you should stop. If you find yourself at the edge of an answer, ask another question. If you want to send what you write back to us, we'd be glad to read it. Some prompts on The Loom will be followed by responses — sometimes from the co-editors, sometimes from readers, sometimes from a guest we've invited to think alongside us. The first thread is yours.
